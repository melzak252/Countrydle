Game Purpose & Gameplay Rules

Countrydle is a daily geography deduction puzzle inspired by 20 Questions and Wordle. Each day at 00:00 UTC, a new mystery country is selected from Countrydle's playable catalog following standard game conventions. Your goal is to deduce the mystery country using yes/no questions and strategic guesses.

Daily Rules & Limits

Question Allowance
10 Questions

Ask up to 10 natural-language yes/no questions. Running out of questions does not end the game if guesses remain.

Guess Allowance
3 Guesses

Submit up to 3 country guesses. Incorrect guesses reveal distance and directional bearing to the secret country.

Country Catalog
195 Countries

Standard playable country pool per Countrydle's game conventions (see mode guide).

Deduction Strategy & Detailed Guide

Effective deduction starts with broad geographic partitions before narrowing down to specific subregions:

  • Hemisphere & Continent: Ask whether the country is in the Northern Hemisphere or within a specific continent.
  • Coastlines & Water: Determine whether the country is landlocked, island-bound, or adjacent to major oceans or seas.
  • Borders & Neighbors: Check whether the target shares a land boundary with pivotal regional neighbors.
