# Product evidence and next-feature decisions

## Decision and baseline status

**Evidence is insufficient to rank new modes, onboarding changes, or duel expansion by measured player friction.** The aggregate-only reporter has been exercised against disposable fixtures and an authorized read-only local database. The local sample is retained development activity, not a production baseline, visitor funnel, traction, retention, causal attribution, or verified answer accuracy. Its small cells remain operator-only evidence rather than published product rates.

The defensible next slice remains reliability and answer-quality work from the hardening assessment, not another mode justified by assumed demand. After a representative authorized baseline, investigate low *observed active-game* completion with gameplay journeys, high report rates with reviewed answer-quality evidence, and duel cancellations/interruptions with retained duel evidence. These remain hypotheses, not observed production friction. Reports are self-selected complaints, not adjudicated errors; completion differences between modes may reflect population or puzzle differences. Do not compare small samples without showing denominators or treat a zero denominator as a 0% rate.

## Metric definitions

All command windows are UTC, `[start, end)` (inclusive start, exclusive end). `observed-until` is an exclusive UTC midnight cutoff for timestamped follow-up and cohort maturity; by default it is today, excluding the current partial day. The command records its actual snapshot generation time. State flags and ownership have no historical timestamp: they are **current snapshot values**, not a reconstruction of their value at `observed-until`. Pinning dates reproduces query definitions, not immutable results after deletion, late updates or sync. Continental daily activity combines all continents. Countrydle, Powiatdle, Wojewodztwodle, US Statedle, Continental and Flagdle states are covered. Hardening adds forward-only persisted Flagdle questions; historical Flagdle question coverage cannot be reconstructed, and this reporter does not invent a Flagdle answer-report cohort. Border Hop has no covered persisted game cohort and is not silently folded into daily metrics.

### First-game completion: desired metric is unknown

Desired definition: among distinct new players whose **first accepted gameplay action** occurs in `[start, end)`, fraction whose first game reaches authoritative win/loss within 24 hours of that action. Denominator is new starters, **not visitors, created states or completed games**. Report guest/account populations at start separately, with explicit sync deduplication; first-visit-to-start conversion would require its own visitor/exposure denominator. A mature 24-hour observation window and authoritative first-start/finish history are absent, so the reporter does not compute this metric.

Available proxy `account_retained_activity_proxies.first_puzzle_date_any_game_completion_snapshot`: denominator accounts whose earliest **retained active puzzle date across the six families** is in `[start, end)`; numerator those with any active game on that date currently marked `is_game_over`. Active means `questions_asked > 0 OR guesses_made > 0`. This counts accounts once, not games; if several modes start on that earliest date it cannot identify which game was first. Earliest retained date is left-censored by deletions, historical coverage and guest-to-account linking; it is not proven first use. It does not establish completion within 24 hours or on the actual first action day.

Supporting `daily_modes.<mode>.account_active_game_completion_snapshot`: numerator currently terminal active account games; denominator active account games on puzzle dates in the window. Games are deduplicated by `(user_id, day_id)` within each mode using `bool_or(is_game_over)`; wins and losses both count. States with no accepted action counters and null account IDs are excluded. This is completion among observed active games, never first-game conversion.

Guests: report unlinked active participation rows, unlinked wins and linked active rows separately. Do not sum linked guest rows with account games (sync can represent the same gameplay twice), infer losses from a missing win, or equate a resettable guest identifier with a person. Guest terminal completion remains explicitly unknown, including for historical rows after integrity hardening adds stronger new-state enforcement.

### Return rate: visitor retention is unknown; puzzle-day account proxy available

Desired definition: among distinct players first starting in `[start, end)`, fraction performing an accepted action on a later UTC date 1–7 days after that first start date. Denominator excludes cohorts whose entire day-7 follow-up is not yet observable. Account-at-start and guest-at-start populations need stable linking and consent/sample accounting; these are not established.

Available `account_retained_activity_proxies.one_to_seven_puzzle_day_return`: denominator earliest-retained-active-date accounts in the window with `first_date + 7 < observed_until`; numerator those with another active puzzle date `> first_date` and `<= first_date + 7`, across any covered daily mode. Same-date play in another mode is not return. Immature accounts are reported and excluded. This is **puzzle-day re-participation**, not visitor or session retention: archive play, delayed sync and puzzle/action-date differences can distort it. No guest return rate is claimed.

### Answer-report rate: answered-question cohort, not report-volume ratio

`daily_modes.<mode>.question_cohort_report_rates`: per question ownership population (`account` for nonnull `user_id`, otherwise `unattributed_or_guest`), denominator persisted questions with `valid = true` and nonnull boolean answer whose `asked_at` is in `[start, end)`; numerator distinct questions in that denominator with an `answer_reports` row matching **both mode and question ID**, submitted before `observed-until`. Invalid/unresolved questions are excluded from this answered denominator; total persisted in-window questions and total reported questions are also displayed to make this exclusion visible. An ordinary boolean false answer is still answered. Rates are zero-to-one fractions, not percentages.

Report submission volume in `[start, end)` is separate and includes reports about older questions. Do not divide it by questions asked in that period. Reporter identity is not question ownership and nullable attribution is not a proven guest population. Null `asked_at` rows are excluded from time-window rates and counted separately across the entire retained question table, alongside total retained questions; empty population maps mean no observed questions, not unknown zero-rate success. Questions deleted while reports survive cannot contribute to a matched-question cohort. Follow-up duration varies for questions within the window, so pin a common cutoff and disclose that right-censoring. No report-control exposure, consent selection, language/device cohort, or proven answer-error rate is available.

### Duel completion: retained creation cohort, seven-day follow-up

`retained_duel_creation_cohort.<population>.seven_day_completion`: denominator retained matches created in `[start, end)` with `created_at + 7 days <= observed_until`; numerator those finished in `[created_at, created_at + 7 days)` with current `status = finished` and result `solved`, `draw`, or `forfeit`. Forfeit is a terminal competitive outcome, not evidence of satisfaction; cancelled lobbies and infrastructure interruptions are not competitive completions. Snapshot cancellation/interruption counts, missing finish timestamps, created totals and immature exclusions are reported separately. These diagnostic snapshot counts can include immature matches and are not denominator subsets for a separate rate.

Populations are current seats: all account-linked, all guest-or-deleted-account, mixed, or unknown/no seats. One seat still contributes a created lobby; it is not a started duel. No creator/visitor/accepted-invitation denominator is implied. Because retention removes completed matches, older retained cohorts can materially understate completion and disproportionately retain unresolved evidence. Prefer an authorized recent window inside the shortest retention boundary, but do not claim that makes historical data complete. Duel report/evidence text is intentionally not read for this metric.

## Read-only baseline command and prerequisites

Run from `server/`, using the supported Python environment and an **explicitly chosen** PostgreSQL asyncpg URL supplied securely through `DATABASE_URL`. The command deliberately does not load `.env` (avoids accidentally selecting the normal player database). Do not put production credentials in shell examples, logs or evidence artifacts.

```sh
# DATABASE_URL must already name the authorized disposable database or read-only reporting source.
python scripts/product_metrics.py \
  --start 2026-09-02 --end 2026-09-30 --observed-until 2026-10-07
```

Required: PostgreSQL, `sqlalchemy` and `asyncpg` from locked backend dependencies, migrated tables for all six daily families, guest participation, daily reports, friend matches and friend seats. No provider, application startup, browser analytics export, signing key, Qdrant or mail settings are needed. Use a least-privilege SELECT-only role where possible. The command creates one repeatable-read **READ ONLY** transaction, sets UTC and a 60-second per-statement timeout, then reads aggregates. It creates no tables, migrates nothing, runs no schedulers, and writes no gameplay, facts, cache or files. Missing schemas/permission/connectivity failures exit nonzero with a sanitized exception class; no partial successful JSON baseline is emitted.

The output is JSON with explicit numerators/denominators, `null` rates when denominators are zero, window metadata, snapshot time, unknown metrics and caveats. It does not persist a baseline automatically. An authorized local read-only run used `[2026-09-02, 2026-09-30)` and follow-up before `2026-10-07`; its aggregate artifact records the actual generation time and caveats. The retained development sample is too small and unrepresentative to rank product work. Production topology, consent configuration, historical deletion coverage, visitor exposure, and production baseline values remain unverified.

## Disposable-data verification contract

Permanent isolated PostgreSQL regressions cover duplicate-state deduplication, untouched states, boolean false versus unresolved answers, cross-mode report IDs, old-question report volume, missing timestamps, day-7 maturity, same-date versus later-date return, and retained duel outcomes/strict completion boundaries. Runtime proof executes the real CLI against disposable fixtures and the separately authorized read-only local source. No live player writes, new tracking, or production-data test fixtures are introduced.

The disposable CLI smoke verified PostgreSQL `READ ONLY`, `REPEATABLE READ`, and UTC settings; an attempted write was denied with SQLSTATE `25006`. Fixture completion was 3/4 active Countrydle account games, return was 1/2 mature earliest-retained-date accounts, and retained account-only duel completion was 2/6. These deliberately constructed boundary cases prove query semantics, not player outcomes. Invalid credentials returned a sanitized error without credential material.

## Collection gaps and approval boundary

First-start and finish timestamps, first-visit/exposure cohorts, durable guest identity/linking, report-control exposure, analytics consent/sample coverage, duel start timestamps and historical deletion coverage remain missing. Decide whether existing operational fields can answer a specific product question before collecting more. Any new telemetry, identifiers, cross-system joins, cohort retention, consent changes, published small-cell output or analytics platform requires a separate scoped implementation and privacy review. H15 does not authorize it.
